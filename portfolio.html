03 / HOW I APPROACH A BUILD

From “what if”
to “give it a try.”

01

Start with the task.

A trip to plan. An answer to find. A story to write. The user's next step gives the interface a purpose.

02

Connect the whole flow.

Bring the interface, model calls, data, and error handling together into something people can use.

03

Keep the person in control.

Make room to review, change, cancel, and try again. Useful software should feel understandable.
